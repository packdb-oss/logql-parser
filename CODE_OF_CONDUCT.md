# Community code of conduct

We welcome participants of every background, identity, experience level and ability.
Be considerate, explain technical disagreement with evidence, accept constructive
feedback, and respect other people's time and privacy. Critique ideas and code, not
people. Help newcomers learn without ridicule.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, impersonation,
retaliation and sustained disruption are unacceptable. This applies in repository
issues, reviews, discussions, events and other spaces where someone represents the
project. Maintainers are subject to the same rules as contributors.

Report concerns privately to conduct@packdb.io. Appeals use the same address with
subject line "Appeal". MAINTAINERS.md states current responder coverage; with one
maintainer there is no independent appeal responder. Share only information
necessary for investigation. Reports are shared only with people who need to
respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.
Do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an inquiry.

Responders acknowledge reports as capacity allows, hear relevant accounts, and
disclose conflicts and seek uninvolved help when available. They may edit/remove
harmful content and apply
proportionate remedies: guidance, a warning, temporary participation limits, or a
permanent ban for serious/repeated harm. Urgent safety action may precede a full
review. Explain decisions privately where practical and document them securely.
An uninvolved responder handles appeals when available; otherwise the limitation
is disclosed to the reporter. Community standing or employer does not exempt
anyone from enforcement.
